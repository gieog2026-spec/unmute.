Our community promise

Speak freely. Respect deeply.

Trust is something a community earns. We want UNMUTE to be built around thoughtful participation and clear safety standards.

  • Respect different experiences and perspectives.
  • Protect privacy and never expose another person's story.
  • No harassment, hate, bullying or spam.
  • Moderation before posts become public.
  • Peer support is not a substitute for professional therapy or medical care.
